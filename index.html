Four workflows, one system

Each part is an n8n workflow. Together they take an application from the form to a decision.

Application form

Applicants fill in the form, and their data is saved to Google Sheets automatically.

CV analysis

AI compares the CV with the job description. The applicant gets an acceptance or rejection email, and HR gets an email with interview questions based on that CV.

Error handling

If any workflow fails, this one catches the error and alerts you.

Ziad HR Assistant

A chatbot on this website that answers HR questions.

What happens after someone applies

If any step fails, the error handling workflow lets you know.

  1. ApplyThe applicant submits the form with their CV.
  2. SaveTheir data is added to Google Sheets.
  3. AnalyzeAI matches the CV to the job description.
  4. NotifyThe applicant gets the result. HR gets suggested interview questions.
